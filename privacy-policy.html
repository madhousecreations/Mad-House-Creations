Privacy Policy

At Mad House Creations, we value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your data when you interact with our website, courses, admissions process, or digital communications.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, course interests, enquiry details, educational background, and communication preferences when you fill out forms, contact us, or request information from us.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information gathered to:

  • Respond to your enrolment or course-related enquiries.
  • Provide information about admissions, fees, schedules, and student support.
  • Improve our website, content, and services.
  • Send relevant updates, newsletters, and promotional information when you have consented.
  • Maintain internal records for academic and operational purposes.

3. Data Protection and Security

We implement reasonable technical and administrative safeguards to protect personal information from unauthorised access, misuse, disclosure, alteration, or loss. However, no system can be completely immune to risks, and we encourage users to exercise caution when sharing personal details online.

4. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies or analytics tools to understand visitor behaviour, improve user experience, and monitor website performance. These tools may collect non-personal information such as browser type, pages visited, and session data. Users can manage cookies through browser settings.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent personal data to third parties. Information may be shared only with trusted service providers who assist us in operating the website, processing enquiries, or delivering course-related services, and only under binding confidentiality obligations.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal or operational requirements. If you wish to withdraw consent or ask for data-related assistance, you may contact us using the details below.
